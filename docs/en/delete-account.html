Deleting your account

Last updated: 23 August 2026

This page describes how to delete your account and the data attached to it in the Na pivo app, in line with the requirements of Google Play and the App Store.

Deleting directly in the app (recommended)

  1. Open the Na pivo app.
  2. Go to Settings (the gear icon, “Nastavení”) → Account (“Účet”).
  3. Choose Delete account (“Smazat účet”) and confirm.

The account is deactivated straight away and you are signed out. Within 14 days you can sign in again and cancel the deletion; after that the account is permanently deleted.

Requesting deletion without the app

If you cannot get into the app, write to us at tomades1@gmail.com from the address you registered with, with the subject “Smazání účtu Na pivo” (Na pivo account deletion). We will handle the request without undue delay.

What gets deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove the data that identifies you and your private content:

  • your email address, name, nickname and profile photo,
  • your password (its hashed form),
  • links to your Google and Apple accounts (we also revoke the Apple token with Apple),
  • your private Beer Diary: the beers you logged, pub ratings and visits,
  • private photos and evenings,
  • social connections: your Parta, friend requests and messages attached to plans,
  • your own public events (meet-ups),
  • contributions about pubs,
  • the feedback you sent us,
  • device sign-in tokens.

What stays

Pub data from other people and from independent sources stays, that is not your account data. Without your identity, only the necessary moderation records and parts of other people’s shared content can remain, for example the technical history of a shared game.

Grace period

Once deletion is requested, the account is deactivated for 14 days and then permanently deleted. If you change your mind, just sign in again within that time and the deletion is cancelled.
