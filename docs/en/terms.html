2. What Na pivo is

Na pivo is a beer diary with a compass. It covers finding pubs nearby, a direction arrow and a map, the Beer Diary (beers logged, visits, ratings, evenings and photos) and a community side: Parta and public profiles, community meet-ups, a photo contest, an AI beer menu scan, leaderboards and badges, and community mapping of pubs (opening hours, beers on tap, amenities).

The core features work without an internet connection; community and synced features need a connection and an account. We keep developing the app, so its look and the range of its features can change.

The app revolves around beer, so one thing bluntly: drink responsibly and never drive under the influence of alcohol. Do not use the app while driving. Counters, leaderboards and badges are statistics and fun, not an encouragement to drink. The app gives no health advice and calls no number of beers safe.
