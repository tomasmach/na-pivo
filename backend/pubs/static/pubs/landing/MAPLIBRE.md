# Map files

`maplibre-gl.mjs`, `maplibre-gl-worker.mjs` and `maplibre-gl.css` are the unmodified
`dist` files of [MapLibre GL JS](https://github.com/maplibre/maplibre-gl-js) 6.16.0
(npm `maplibre-gl@6.16.0`), BSD 3-Clause licence, copyright MapLibre contributors.

`map-style.json` is the OpenFreeMap `dark` style recoloured to the app's gray ground
and amber accent, without its sprite. The vector tiles and glyphs it points at come
from tiles.openfreemap.org, built from OpenStreetMap data (ODbL); the pages credit both.
